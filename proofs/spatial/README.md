# The transfer rules of the resource analysis: what is proved, what is checked, what is a premise

Spatial soundness must be a property of the domain, not an empirical inference from the benchmark
corpus. This directory holds the part of that which is not a Lean theorem:
the registry of every transfer rule ([REGISTRY.tsv](REGISTRY.tsv)), the independent checker
([check_transfers.py](check_transfers.py)), its inputs and outputs (`out/`, generated by
`sbt "testOnly morkl.SpatialTransferDump"`, not committed) and its verdicts
([STATUS.tsv](STATUS.tsv), regenerated by the checker; `scripts/proof_closure.py --check` refuses an
`OPEN` row).  The Lean half is [proofs/lean/Zippy/Spatial.lean](../lean/Zippy/Spatial.lean).

## 1. The statement each rule must satisfy

A transfer rule maps abstract inputs to an abstract result.  Its soundness statement is the one the
task gives: *the concretization of the abstract result contains every concrete event execution
admitted by the abstract inputs*.  Two readings of "execution" are in play:

- **values** — the correlated domain's operations (`Domain.union`, …, `Domain.range`) must satisfy
  γ(op#(x, y)) ⊇ { op(a, b) | a ∈ γ(x), b ∈ γ(y) }; on the EXACT tier γ is a singleton and the
  statement is equality (the operation is exact);
- **events** — the pricing of a constructor on a backend must satisfy, for every admitted
  execution, lo ≤ counted ≤ hi on every event.

## 2. What Lean proves (`Spatial.lean`)

The arithmetic the pricing composes with — `add`, `mul`, `hull`, repetition (`sum_mem`), alternatives
(`mem_foldl_hull`) — and the containment order on intervals; the MUST/MAY rule (`must_may`: a lower
endpoint below every admitted execution and an upper endpoint above every one contain them all);
`RangeBounds.normalize` transcribed with its window and slice-length lemmas; the widening contract
(`widen_upper`); and the finite-model principle (`exhaustive`).  These are the lemmas every rule's
soundness argument reduces to once the rule's own case analysis is done.

## 3. What the checker checks, and the argument that connects it to the general rule

`check_transfers.py` re-derives every exact-tier value transfer with its own path-set algebra over
the **whole small universe** — paths over {a, b} of length ≤ 2, at most three per value: 64 values,
4096 ordered pairs per binary operation, 8 windows per value for `range` — and requires equality with
the domain's result.  It also re-checks the arithmetic of containment on the dumped pricing rows.  Its
coverage check refuses a dump that misses a pair.

**Why a small universe decides more than itself.**  Every exact-tier transfer in
`SpatialDomain.scala` is defined by structural recursion on the two tries: at a node it decides the
terminal flag from the two terminal flags, and for each key it either descends into the pair of
children (a key present on both sides), copies one child by pointer (present on one side), or drops
it.  The decision at a node depends only on the *local configuration* — the two terminal flags and,
per key, which sides carry it — and the recursion below is the same transfer on smaller tries.  The
small universe contains every local configuration: two heads (`a`, `b`) give every key pattern
(both / left / right / neither), depth 2 gives a node whose child is itself non-trivial, and up to
three paths per value give every combination of terminal flags with those patterns.  So a transfer
that is exact on every pair of universe values is exact at every node configuration, and by
induction on the height of the tries it is exact on every pair of tries — the universe is the base
case of the induction, and the inductive step is the shape of the recursion itself.  What the argument
does **not** cover is a transfer whose result depends on something outside the local configuration:
`range`, which is why `range` is checked over eight windows including the identity, empty, first,
last and negative ones and why its normalisation is proved in Lean rather than checked.

For the **pricing rows** the checker is an independent re-check of the arithmetic on the Scala
numbers (a row's counted total against its interval).  The counted totals are produced by the
instrumented executors, which are the operational semantics of the language; that the compositional
event semantics reproduces them is checked differentially by `SpatialSemanticsCheck` and is
registered as the trusted boundary **T9** (`docs/TRUSTED.md`).  The exhaustive containment gates
(`SpatialCostCheck`, `SpatialEventsCheck`, `SpatialScaleCheck`) are the full enumeration; the
checker's rows are its spot re-check by a second implementation of the comparison.

## 4. What remains a premise

`REGISTRY.tsv` lists three: the `Long` arithmetic is the `WithTop ℕ` arithmetic below 2^63
(A6-IVL-IMPL); the abstract iteration's termination is a checked run-time property with an
infinite report on failure (A6-BUDGET); the Patricia shape depends on the interner and is priced by
an envelope that holds for every id assignment (A6-PATRICIA).  A cost result is **certified**
(`CostReport.certified`) only when every rule its derivation used is `PROVED` in `STATUS.tsv`; the
premises are `CHECKED-PREMISE` rows and are listed in the report's dependencies.

## 5. Running it

```
sbt "testOnly morkl.SpatialTransferDump"      # writes proofs/spatial/out/{transfers,bounds}.tsv
python3 proofs/spatial/check_transfers.py     # re-derives, re-checks, writes STATUS.tsv
scripts/check_lean.sh                         # builds Spatial.lean with the rest of the package
python3 scripts/proof_closure.py --check      # refuses an OPEN transfer row
```
